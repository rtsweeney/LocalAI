Setup: connect ComfyUI and add your workflows

Images, video and music are made by ComfyUI. A workflow is ComfyUI's saved recipe for one kind of job. You pick a recipe that works on your card once, and AI Home reuses it with your own prompts and photos.

For each: in ComfyUI open Workflow > Browse Templates, pick the template, let it download its models, press Run once to see it work, then choose Workflow > Export (API) and add that file here.

LoRAs

What is a LoRA? A small add-on file (usually 20 to 300 MB) that teaches an image or video model one new thing: a particular person, a drawing style, a type of object. The big model stays the same; the LoRA nudges it.

Each LoRA is made for one model family (a FLUX LoRA won't work on SDXL), and most need a trigger word in your prompt, listed on the page you got it from. Strength is how hard it pushes: 1.0 is normal, lower is subtler.

Only .safetensors files from huggingface.co and civitai.com are accepted. Older formats can hide program code.

Characters

A character is a LoRA trained on photos of one person, so that person can appear in new images and videos. This step prepares the photos: it removes duplicates, checks sizes, and writes a caption for each with the vision model.

Training is a separate step done in a trainer program; the guide explains it. Use 20 to 30 photos with different angles, distances, outfits, expressions and backgrounds.

3D models (CAD)

Describe an object with sizes. A model writes the design as OpenSCAD code, OpenSCAD builds it, and you get an STL file for a 3D printer plus the editable source. Good for brackets, stands, boxes and simple parts.

Make music

Describe the style, add lyrics if you want singing, and choose a length. Leave lyrics empty for an instrumental.

Mark sections with [verse], [chorus] and [bridge] on their own lines.

Your tracks

What is a plugin? A small program that gives your AI agents new abilities, such as reading web pages or taking software apart. Plugins speak a standard called MCP.

AI Home runs a plugin hub. You connect the chat page and the app builder to the hub once. After that, every plugin you add here, and AI Home's own tools below, are available to both.

Scheduled jobs

Each job runs on its own, one at a time, and saves what it produced. A model job starts fresh every run: it remembers nothing from earlier runs or chats, so write the request so it stands alone.

New job

Runs in the LocalAI folder, the same as typing it into a Command Prompt window. Only use commands you understand.
